Shipping and delivery policy

  • Processing time: All orders are processed and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days after order confirmation.
  • Delivery time: Standard delivery across India takes 4 to 7 business days, depending on your location and pin code.
  • Shipping charges: Delivery is free when you pay online. A flat delivery charge applies to each Cash on delivery order, and it is shown in your bag before you order. Orders above the free-delivery amount shown in your bag get free delivery.
  • Tracking: Tracking details are shared by SMS, WhatsApp or email once your order is shipped.

Return, exchange and refund policy

At RUDRA FASHION, we want you to love what you wear. If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong product or size, we offer an easy 7-day return or exchange.

  • Eligibility: Items must be unused, unwashed and in original condition with all tags intact. An unboxing video is mandatory to claim returns for damaged or missing items.
  • Return process: To request a return or exchange, contact us within 7 days of delivery at rudrashekhawat13686@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 92510 73424 with your Order ID and photos or video.
  • Refund timeline: Once we receive and inspect the returned product, your refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days to your original payment method (UPI or bank account).

Privacy policy

At RUDRA FASHION, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. When you visit our website or place an order, we collect basic details such as your name, shipping address, phone number and email address solely to process and deliver your orders.

Online payments are made through UPI and trusted payment apps. We do not store your card details, UPI PIN or bank passwords. We never sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party, except our courier partners who need it to deliver your order.

For any privacy-related query, write to rudrashekhawat13686@gmail.com.
